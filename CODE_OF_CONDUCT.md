# Code of Conduct

This is a small project; the rules are short.

**Expected:** Be direct and technical. Critique code, not people. Assume good
faith — especially across languages and time zones. Help newcomers land their
first PR.

**Not tolerated:** Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks,
sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private information. Anywhere the
project lives — issues, PRs, discussions, commits.

**Enforcement:** Report conduct issues privately to
**matosevic.markom@gmail.com**. The maintainer reviews every report; outcomes
range from a warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion.
Reports are kept confidential.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
